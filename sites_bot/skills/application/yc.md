# YC role application

On a ycombinator.com company job page, use the role's **Apply to role** link.
The header's generic **Apply** is a founder funding application, not a job application.
Never use the founder application. A role link may open a candidate dialog on the
same page or continue to Work at a Startup; inspect the actual result.

An open application dialog is not evidence that the candidate is signed in.
Check the account state and use the saved candidate session when available. If
Work at a Startup requires sign-in, report the sign-in page for the user rather
than creating a new YC account. Do not claim the login worked while Log in is shown.

Fill name, contact details, location, links and resume only from the supplied facts.
Choose the phone dial code from the saved international phone number and inspect
the displayed number before continuing. After a location selection or resume
upload, re-read the form because YC may replace its controls. A short motivation
answer can be written from the candidate's experience and this role's posting.

Stop for CAPTCHA or another human check without interacting with it. Report the
exact role URL and what remains. Opening **Apply to role** is navigation, not a
submission; report submission_attempted=true if the actual Submit was clicked or
its outcome is uncertain. Verify a role-specific receipt before reporting submitted.

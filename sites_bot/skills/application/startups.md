# Startup specialist (Y Combinator and Hacker News "Who is hiring?")
The task's job came from a YC job post or a Hacker News hiring comment, not from a job board. The
news.ycombinator.com link is only where the post was found: never post, comment, vote or sign in there.
Apply on the startup's own route: an Ashby, Lever, Greenhouse, Workable or YC Work at a Startup page,
or the company's careers page. Check that the company and role on the page match the post; one comment
often lists several roles, so pick the role the task names, or the closest match it names, and report it.
Startup forms ask short free-text questions ("why us", "what have you built", "anything else"): answer
them from saved experience and what this startup's post and site say it builds, following the form
answer rules. A missing salary, visa, start date or location answer goes in `missing`, never a guess.
Work at a Startup needs a YC account: if it is not already signed in, stop and report it rather than
create one. If the post says to email the founders or a hiring address, report `email_application`
with that exact address instead of searching the site for another contact.
Verify a receipt page or confirmation message for this role before reporting success.
